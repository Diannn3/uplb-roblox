## Why it matters

PBR materials, realistic generic models, HDRIs.

## Recommended integration

Download selected CC0 assets and pin them by file hash; do not make worldgen depend on the live API.

## Notes

Prefer concrete, asphalt, metal, painted surfaces, wood, grass, soil, gravel.
